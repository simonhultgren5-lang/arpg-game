# Depths of Darkness: the four acts

A grim gothic descent in four acts. Each act goes one layer further down, from a cursed town into the ground beneath it, through a burning waste and finally into the abyss itself. The tone is quiet dread that breaks into violence: candlelight, wet stone, ash, and people who stayed too long.

**The core mystery:** beneath Hollowmere lies the Maw, a wound in the world that the founders sealed by walling thirteen living saints into the rock around it. The saints' prayers kept the seal shut for three centuries. Now the prayers are failing, and something below has learned to ring the bells that once called the faithful to worship. Every toll calls the dead upward and pulls the living down.

**The hero** is a stranger who walks out of the fog on the old road. The road behind them never comes back. Late in the story it is hinted (never confirmed) that the fog brings people to Hollowmere when the seal needs a new saint.

| Act | Place | Boss | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Hollowmere, the cursed town, and the Sunken Crypt | Hask, the Bell-Warden | Something tolls below |
| II | The Ossuary of the Thirteen (deep catacombs) | Mother Ossory, the Knuckle-Saint | Faith that rotted into hunger |
| III | The Cinder Reach (burning wasteland) | Varrow, the Ash Regent | What the fire was hiding |
| IV | The Maw (the abyss) | The Unlit | The last saint |

## Act I: The Tolling of Hollowmere

*Implemented in the Godot slice (the opening quest, "The Silent Bell").*

**Setting.** A walled mining town on a black mere, held in fog. St. Aldric's chapel stands at its north end with a bell tower and no bell. East of the chapel is a graveyard full of disturbed earth and the Mausoleum of the Wardens, whose gate leads down to the Sunken Crypt: flooded halls where the chapel's wardens were buried standing in their armor.

**NPCs**
- **Sister Maud Ashcombe**, last sister of St. Aldric's. Brisk, exhausted, faith worn down to habit. Quest giver for the act. Knows more about the founders than she says.
- **Edda Varge**, the smith. Her forge has gone cold; she brews grave-moss draughts (healing potions) instead. Her brother came back three nights after his burial and stood outside her window until dawn.
- **Old Corwin**, the gravedigger. Has dug most graves in Hollowmere twice. Gives the hero his lantern-charm. Gallows humour, real fear underneath.
- **The Lamplighter** *(in game)*, a child who walks the town at night lighting lamps no one fuelled. Speaks only in questions. Recurs in every act.

**Quest beats**
1. **The Silent Bell** *(in game)*. Every night the bell of St. Aldric's tolls thirteen times, though the bell was cut down and smashed. Maud sends the hero through the mausoleum gate to find what rings below.
2. **The Sunken Crypt** *(in game)*. A procedurally assembled crypt of halls, pillared naves, ossuaries and flooded chapels. Hollows (risen townsfolk), Grave Crawlers and Bone Archers.
3. **Boss: Hask, the Bell-Warden** *(in game)*. A huge warden buried in the bell-pit holding the old bell's clapper as penance; the clapper is now fused into his chest. He tolls it to send shockwaves through the pit, and halfway through the fight the toll raises the dead to his side. Drops **the Black Clapper**.
4. **The Thirteen Names** *(in game)*. Maud takes the clapper. It is warm and whispers thirteen names: the saints walled up beneath the town. Hask was not ringing to wake Hollowmere; he was ringing to wake *them*, and something deeper was ringing back.
5. **Lamps Without Oil** *(in game)*. Once the bell is silent, the Lamplighter waits in the square and asks the hero to follow their lamps. The lamps light a trail through the graveyard to an unmarked grave. It holds the Founders' Charter, which names the thirteen saints and calls them "the Seal". Reward: the Lamplighter's Wick.
6. **The Wardens' Oath** *(in game, optional)*. Old Corwin explains that the wardens can't rest without their names. Three name-stones lie in the Sunken Crypt; set back on the plinth by the mausoleum, they lay the wardens to rest. Reward: the Warden's Vigil, one of the Act I unique items (with the Lamplighter's Wick, Hask's Toll and the Knuckle of the Thirteenth).

**Act transition** *(in game)*. When Maud reads the charter, the town's wells begin to toll. She opens the chapel's sealed undercroft, where a stair leads far below the crypt into the Ossuary; going down it ends Act I.

## Act II: The Ossuary of the Thirteen

**Setting.** Catacombs older than the town: bone-lined galleries, reliquary chapels, a drowned cathedral, and thirteen walled cells around a central shaft. Each cell holds a saint, some still praying, some long since turned to something else.

**NPCs**
- **Brother Ilfric**, a monk who crawled into the Ossuary forty years ago to pray with the saints and never left. Blind, kind, entirely mad. Merchant of relics.
- **Saint Wenna the Unbroken**, the one saint still truly praying. Speaks to the hero through the wall of her cell and asks them to keep her alive long enough to finish her prayer.
- **Maud**, who follows the hero down. Her faith cracks as she sees what the saints have become.

**Quest beats**
1. **The Undercroft Stair**. Descend; the first cells are open and empty, with claw marks on the inside.
2. **Thirteen Cells**. The hub quest: visit the saints. Each cell is a short dungeon branch. Some saints can be released into true death (merciful), some must be fought, some must be left alone because their prayer still holds a piece of the seal.
3. **The Choir of Teeth**. A cathedral where the risen faithful still sing; the song is a spell. Silence the three choir-masters.
4. **What Wenna Saw**. Wenna reveals that the saints were not volunteers. The founders chose people who had walked out of the fog.
5. **Boss: Mother Ossory, the Knuckle-Saint**. The first saint to break. She ate her own prayers, then the others' bones, and wears a cathedral of knuckles. Phases: the reliquary, the choir, the hunger.

**Act transition.** With Ossory dead, the shaft at the centre of the Ossuary cracks open. Heat pours upward. Wenna's prayer falters. Maud stays behind to pray in her place.

## Act III: The Cinder Reach

**Setting.** Below the Ossuary the ground opens onto a vast cavern-sky over a burning waste: ash dunes, rivers of slag, the ruins of a city that fell into the Maw centuries before Hollowmere was built. The founders of Hollowmere came from here.

**NPCs**
- **Kessa Embervane**, a scavenger-captain who leads a caravan of survivors on iron sledges. Pragmatic, funny, utterly ruthless.
- **The Ash Choir**, ghosts of the old city who remember being burned and try to warn the hero, but can only speak in fire.
- **The Lamplighter** again, now an adult, now old, always lighting lamps.

**Quest beats**
1. **Fall into Fire**. Survive the descent; meet Kessa's caravan.
2. **The Charter's Other Half**. The old city's archive holds the original pact: the city tried to seal the Maw with fire, failed, and fled upward to try again with saints.
3. **Three Furnaces**. Kessa needs three furnace-cores to take the caravan home. Each furnace is guarded by a cinder lord who keeps a burning piece of the old seal.
4. **Betrayal on the Slag Road**. Kessa sells the hero's location to the Ash Regent for safe passage. She can be spared later; the choice echoes in Act IV.
5. **Boss: Varrow, the Ash Regent**. The last king of the old city, kept alive by the furnace-seal for six hundred years. He wants to burn everything above to keep the Maw fed. Phases: the throne, the bellows, the ash storm.

**Act transition.** Varrow's death extinguishes the furnaces. With no fire and almost no prayer left, the Maw opens beneath the city.

---

## Act IV: The Maw

**Setting.** The abyss. Inverted cathedrals hanging from nothing, stairs that descend into themselves, the bells of every drowned town tolling at once. Light works differently here: torches burn black, and the hero's own light is the only true one.

**NPCs**
- **Maud**, or what reaches the Maw of her. She finished Wenna's prayer and became the fourteenth saint; she can guide the hero but cannot leave.
- **Kessa** (if spared), who comes down to pay her debt.
- **The Lamplighter**, revealed as the first hero who walked out of the fog and refused to become a saint. They have been lighting the way for every hero since, hoping one would end it instead of sealing it.

**Quest beats**
1. **The Stair That Descends Into Itself**. A loop that only breaks when the hero stops trying to go back up.
2. **The Bells of Every Town**. Silence the bells of the other drowned towns; each is a short memory-dungeon of a place the Maw already ate.
3. **The Fourteenth Saint**. Maud asks the hero to take her place so she can die. The Lamplighter asks the hero to refuse.
4. **Boss: The Unlit**. The thing in the Maw: not a demon but an absence that learned to wear what falls into it. It wears Hask, then Ossory, then Varrow, then the hero's own shape. Final phase in total darkness: only the hero's light radius exists.

**Endings**
- **The Seal.** The hero becomes the new saint. Hollowmere's bells fall silent for three hundred years. The fog takes the road again.
- **The Ending.** With the Lamplighter's lamp, the hero lights the Maw itself and burns the Unlit out. Hollowmere sinks into the mere; the fog lifts for the first time; there is no one left to see it.
- *(secret)* **The Bell.** If Kessa was spared and every warden laid to rest, the hero can recast the Black Clapper into a new bell and toll it from the bottom of the Maw, waking every saint at once to hold the seal together, and walk back up the road out of the fog.
